Appointment letters you can trust

Every letter can be checked in seconds

Each appointment gets a public ID such as BSU-K7Q2X9 and a private verification token. Both are printed on your letter, and the QR code takes anyone straight to the check page.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an account to book?

Yes. An account lets you manage appointments, receive reminders and download letters. Creating one takes under a minute.

How do I print my appointment letter?

Open the appointment and select Download letter (PDF). The PDF opens in your browser, where you can print it or save it to your phone.

Can I change or cancel an appointment?

Yes, up to {{ cutoff_hours }} hours before the visit. After that, please call the clinic for help.

What if someone asks me to verify my letter?

Anyone can check an appointment at the verification page by entering its ID or token, or by scanning the QR code. Patient names are partly hidden on that page.

In an emergency, what should I do?

Do not wait for an online appointment. Go to the nearest emergency unit or call your local emergency number straight away.
